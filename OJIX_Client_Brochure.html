The Problem

Property Due Diligence Is Broken

The same property fact can appear under different names, spellings, survey subdivisions, and document formats — leaving lawyers, buyers and consultants with fragmented information and no durable evidence trail.

📄

Scattered Documents

Sale deeds, RTCs, ECs, e-Khata, mutation extracts — spread across portals, paper files, and email threads with no single source of truth.

🗺️

Hidden Spatial Risks

Rajakaluve encroachments, lake buffer violations and planning restrictions are discovered too late — often after a transaction is committed.

🔗

Broken Title Chains

Missing parent deeds, date gaps and conflicting ownership claims across 30+ years of transaction history go undetected in manual review.

⏱️

Weeks of Manual Work

Lawyers and analysts spend days cross-referencing names, survey numbers and extents across instruments — with no audit trail of what was checked.

Karnataka-First Design

Built for How Property Works in Karnataka

Not a generic document tool. OJIX understands Karnataka's land record system, measurement units, government portals, and legal terminology.

  • ✓ Full Location Hierarchy — District → Taluk → Hobli → Village dropdown system aligned with Karnataka land records
  • ✓ Karnataka Land Units — Native support for Acres, Guntas, Sq. Ft., Sq. Meters with accurate cross-conversions
  • ✓ Karnataka Document Types — Classifiers for Sale Deed, RTC/Pahani, EC, e-Khata, Mutation Extract, Gift Deed, and more
  • ✓ Rajakaluve / SWD Buffer Rules — 50m primary, 25m secondary, and 15m tertiary buffer screening for storm-water drains
  • ✓ Lake & Water Body Screening — 30m buffer enforcement around lake and wetland peripheries
  • ✓ Alias & Transliteration — Party name normalisation for Kannada / English transliteration variations
