1. Executive summary
OJIX Property Intelligence is proposed as a single, reusable intelligence platform for lawyers, property
consultants, developers, due-diligence teams and serious buyers who need to answer one question quickly:
“What do we know about this property, what evidence supports it, what is inconsistent, what could affect the
transaction, and what requires professional review?”
The concept consolidates the requirements discussed around legal office automation, property due diligence,
survey and parcel maps, Rajakaluve / storm-water-drain risk, lakes and water bodies, land-use and
development-plan checks, title-chain reconstruction, OCR, government notifications, case-law research,
structured legal reporting and subscription-based delivery.

2. Product vision, problem and objectives
2.1 Problem statement

Property due diligence is fragmented across documents, portals, maps, local knowledge, manual searches
and professional judgment.

Critical signals are often spatial: survey boundaries, drains, lakes, canals, roads, planning designations and
proposed infrastructure.

The same fact may appear under different names, spellings, survey subdivisions, owner names or
document formats.

Traditional workflows do not provide a durable evidence trail showing which source supported each
finding and when it was checked.

AI can speed extraction and research, but ungrounded output is unacceptable for high-impact property
decisions.

3.1 Operating model

Tenant -> Workspace -> Matter -> Property -> Sources -> Evidence -> Findings -> Tasks -> Report

Every derived fact has provenance. Every high-impact rule has a reason, inputs, rule version and reviewer
state.


4. Scope and product boundaries
4.1 In scope for MVP

Karnataka / Bengaluru-first property dossier and matter management.

Property identity fields: district, taluk, hobli, village, survey number, subdivision, site / property ID where
applicable, extent, address and coordinates.

Document upload and classification for common property/legal documents.

OCR + structured extraction + evidence anchors.

Title-chain workspace and owner/instrument timeline.

Cross-document mismatch detection.

GIS risk screening for configured layers such as survey parcel geometry, storm-water drain / Rajakaluve,
lakes/waterbodies, planning and road/infrastructure overlays where licensed/available.

Legal research workspace with citations and source links.

Risk register and explainable findings.

Reviewable PDF/HTML report generation.

RBAC, audit logs, tenant isolation, retention controls and operational monitoring.
4.2 Explicitly out of scope / requires later authority or commercial integrations

Issuing any government certificate.

Automatically declaring legal ownership, legality, encroachment or title validity.

Unapproved scraping or bypassing of government portals, CAPTCHAs or authentication controls.

Automated legal representation before a court or authority.

Native mobile apps as a first requirement; PWA is the baseline.

Country-wide cadastral completeness on day one.

Large-scale litigation management before the property intelligence foundation is stable.

32.2 Final product principle

followed back to the source artifact, every rule result to a versioned rule, every AI statement to retrieved
evidence, and every final conclusion to a named reviewer.
